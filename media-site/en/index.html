GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Plans, AI, and data at a glance.

Contact support ↗
What is the difference between the three plans?

Free supports 3 accounts and 1 configuration. Standard and Pro each support 50 accounts and 8 configurations.

How is AI usage billed?

Built-in AI costs 1 point per successful generation or rewrite. For your own API, you pay the provider directly.

Are drafts and assets uploaded to HIITUN’s cloud?

Drafts and assets stay on-device; platforms and AI connect when you act. Account and subscription data follow the Privacy Policy.

How do I sign in?

Select “Log in” and use the HIITUN account email and password from the desktop app.
